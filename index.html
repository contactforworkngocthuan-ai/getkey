Roblox Key System

Get your access key through the in-game mobile menu. The key is linked to the Roblox UserId that started the request.

  1. 01Tap GET KEYThe Roblox menu creates your one-time Link4m URL.
  2. 02Complete Link4mOpen the short link and continue to the claim page.
  3. 03Verify you're humanComplete the hCaptcha check on the claim page.
  4. 04Copy and verify your keyPaste the key back into the Roblox menu.

Keys expire automatically. Do not share your key. The claim ticket expires after 15 minutes and can only be used once.
